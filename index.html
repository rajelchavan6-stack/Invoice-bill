Invoice type & tax options
Most shops use Tax Invoice. Pick Bill of Supply if you're under the composition scheme or sell GST-exempt goods (no GST shown). Pick Export for sales outside India.
Note: If your turnover is above ₹5 crore, the law also requires e-invoicing (IRN + QR code from the government portal). This tool doesn't generate IRN/QR, so larger businesses should use a registered e-invoicing system for B2B bills.
